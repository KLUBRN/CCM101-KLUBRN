# Reflection

**1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?**

The cloud platform that stood out to me the most was **AWS**. I was impressed by the wide range of services it offers, including computing, storage, databases, networking, and security. AWS also seems flexible enough to support individual developers, small businesses, and large enterprises. Learning about its different tools made me interested in exploring the platform further.

**2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?**

AWS, Azure, and GCP all provide similar cloud computing solutions. Each platform offers virtual servers, cloud storage, databases, networking tools, security features, identity management, and Kubernetes support. They can all help organizations run applications, manage data, and scale their systems. Their main differences are related to pricing, service names, features, and their individual strengths.

**3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?**

Business needs play a major role in choosing a cloud provider. Organizations must evaluate their budget, security expectations, performance requirements, scalability, current software, and long-term goals. For instance, a business that depends heavily on Microsoft applications may prefer Azure. On the other hand, a company focused on data analytics or artificial intelligence might choose GCP because of its strengths in those areas.

**4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?**

This activity taught me that selecting a cloud platform requires more than choosing the provider with the biggest reputation. A Cloud Solutions Consultant must first understand the client's objectives, technical environment, budget, and future plans. I learned how to compare cloud services and create a recommendation that matches a client's specific requirements.

**5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?**

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has become more complete and organized. I added information from my Linux investigation, cloud platform research, service comparisons, and client recommendations. The portfolio now demonstrates my understanding of AWS, Azure, and GCP. It also shows that I can connect technical concepts to practical business situations.
